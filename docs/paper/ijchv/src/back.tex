\section*{Data availability}
Both anatomical cohorts are public, de-identified deposits: the Roney
left-atrial virtual cohort (Zenodo 5801337) and the University of
Washington/Boyle cohort (Dryad \code{10.5061/dryad.kkwh70sg0}). Neither deposit
contains an outcome label; the two-year recurrence outcomes were withheld from
the public download and obtained by direct request to the cohort's authors, who
have confirmed in writing that no confidentiality attaches to them. Analysis
code, the frozen pre-registration, and all result files are publicly available
in the project repository.
