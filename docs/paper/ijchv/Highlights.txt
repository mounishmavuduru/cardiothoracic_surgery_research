Highlights

- A spectral fragility index added no useful prediction of atrial reentry
- In 182 patient-derived atria the gain was +0.012 AUC, below the pre-registered 0.05 gate
- A positive clinical result was an artefact that Gaussian noise reproduced
- The first-order estimator is ill-conditioned on real atrial meshes
- Removing any single pre-registered guard admitted a specific false positive
